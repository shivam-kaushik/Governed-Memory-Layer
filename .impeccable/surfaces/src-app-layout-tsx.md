## Scope
Whole app (Chat, Monitor, Memories, Audit, Proofs, side rail). Mode: Operate.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Memory activity is made visible as glass in space: KPIs are translucent 3D columns with floating sample points, and a 3D keeper character (Mnemo) reacts to every memory change. Refuses the flat white-card admin dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Deep ink-blue ground, translucent glass panels, five fixed tone colours (teal saved, violet agent, amber updated, rose forgotten or denied, blue data), Geist type, perspective grid floor, gyroscope logo.
STORY: A responder or judge sees at a glance what the agent recalled, saved, updated or forgot, with the numbers and sample counts in plain text beside every 3D view.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass rail left (icon nav with sliding pill, Mnemo dock, session picker). Main: page title, then on Monitor a wide 3D KPI stage over a six-card legend; on Chat Mnemo above the headline and example prompts.
FORM: pinned by the user's words ("3d components", "3d characters", "kpis moving 3d ... transparency and data points", loading animation), seed: none (pinned).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
